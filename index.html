Support

Questions, a chart that looks wrong, or something not working? Email mediavanity@gmail.com.

Report a chart error

Open the tune and tap Report a chart error under the chart, or email the song title, the bar numbers and what you think the chord should be. A recording you're following helps too. Players and recordings vary, so some differences are deliberate, but every report gets checked.

Common questions

How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Tap the i button at the top of the song list, then Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought Pro with.

Playback is silent.

Turn up the volume with the side buttons while a tune is playing, and check that the sound isn't going to a Bluetooth speaker or headphones connected elsewhere.

How do I keep my own songs safe?

Your songs are stored on your device. In the About screen (the i button), tap Back up my songs to save them to a file, and Restore from a file to bring them back on any device.

Can I play along on a guitar tuned down?

Yes. Open View options and set Playback tuning. It shifts only the sound, so the chart and diagrams stay as written.

About the charts

Each chart is the common-practice harmony for the tune: the changes jazz musicians widely play on it, written out independently for this app. No melodies or lyrics are included. Titles are used only to identify the tunes; each composition belongs to its composers and publishers.
