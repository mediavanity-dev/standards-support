Privacy policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Standards collects no data. The app has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and it doesn't send anything about you or your use of it to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Songs you add and your settings, such as your last tune, tempo and display choices, are saved only on your device. They're never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them. If you use Back up my songs, the file goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

Purchases

Standards Pro is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your payment details or Apple Account information. The app checks with Apple only whether Pro is unlocked.

Email

If you email us, for support or to report a chart error, we use your message and email address only to reply. We don't add you to any list or share your email.

Children

Standards collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
